# Community and candidate distinctions

## Community
r/leagueoflegends (public, no login needed)

## What I read
About 46 posts across Hot, New, and Top (this week and this month),
read top to bottom.

## Candidate distinctions
1. **Evidence vs. assertion.** Some posts back a claim with specifics
   (the Maokai post cites Lolalytics tier, pickrate, and winrate; the Diadem of Songs
   post cites pick rate and win rate). Others make a strong claim with nothing
   behind it (the Yone nerf post, the Mel rework post).
2. **Asking vs. arguing.** Many posts just ask the community something
   ("What's a champion you don't understand why anyone would main?").
   Others take a side (the smurf ban post, Yasuo vs. Yone).
3. **Personal story vs. game content.** Some posts are about the poster's
   life, not the game (the anxiety medication post, the "uninstalled League" post).
4. **Help-seeking.** New is full of posts where one person wants a personal
   answer (LP question, beginner champ recs, "struggling to find my role").

## Posts I'm skipping
Image-only, video-only, and esports schedule/update posts.
